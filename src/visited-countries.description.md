parkrun Countries Visited shows country flag emojis for every country where a parkrunner has finished, on their all-results page (e.g. [parkrunner A1179622](https://www.parkrun.org.uk/parkrunner/1179622/all/)).

## How countries are inferred

The country is taken from the event link’s hostname (for example `.com.au` → Australia, `.org.uk` → United Kingdom, `.co.nz` → New Zealand). Domain suffixes do not always match political boundaries; a short disclaimer appears with the summary.

## What you see

Flags beside each event in the results table, plus a `#countries-visited` summary under the parkrunner heading. Hover a flag for the country name and finish count.
