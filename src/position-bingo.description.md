parkrun Position Bingo Challenge tracks the unofficial position bingo challenge on a parkrunner’s all-results page (e.g. [parkrunner A1965346](https://www.parkrun.org.uk/parkrunner/1965346/all/)).

## Challenge rules

Each finish contributes the **last two digits** of the finish position (00–99). Results are read oldest-first. Every hit is recorded; the grid cell shows the **first** time that square was filled. Scanning stops once all 100 squares are filled.

## What you see

A 10×10 bingo grid. Click a completed square for a popup listing the finishes that landed on it.

**Save as Image** downloads a square tile sized for sharing on social media, including activity sites such as Strava.
