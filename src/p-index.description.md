parkrun p-index display calculates the unofficial parkrun p-index on a parkrunner’s all-results page (e.g. [parkrunner A1179626](https://www.parkrun.org.uk/parkrunner/1179626/all/)).

## What is the p-index?

The p-index is the largest whole number _n_ such that you have completed at least _n_ different events at least _n_ times each. For example, a p-index of 10 means at least ten events with ten or more finishes apiece.

## What you see

The p-index value plus the contributing events (finish counts and the date/event-number window that established each contribution).

**Save as Image** downloads the display.
