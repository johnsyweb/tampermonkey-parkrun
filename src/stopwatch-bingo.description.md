parkrun Stopwatch Bingo visualises the unofficial stopwatch bingo challenge on a parkrunner’s all-results page (e.g. [parkrunner A4886000](https://www.parkrun.org.uk/parkrunner/4886000/all/)).

## Challenge rules

Each finish contributes the **seconds** portion of the finish time (00–59), from `MM:SS` or `HH:MM:SS`. Results are read oldest-first; the first hit for each second is kept. The challenge is complete when all 60 seconds are collected.

## What you see

A clock-style display of collected seconds. Segment length reflects how often each second appears. Click a segment for finish details.

**Save as Image** downloads a square tile sized for sharing on social media, including activity sites such as Strava.
