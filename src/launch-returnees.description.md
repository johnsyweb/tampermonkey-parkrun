parkrun Launch Returnees lists finishers who were at both the location’s launch event (event #1) and the results page you are viewing (e.g. [Cruickshank Park results](https://www.parkrun.com.au/cruickshankpark/results/2026-02-28/)).

## Where it works

It runs on **dated** single-event results URLs. It does not run on `latestresults` pages or on the launch results page itself.

## What you see

A scrollable list of people who appear in both the launch field and the current results, with links to their parkrunner profiles.

## Privacy and data

The script fetches the public launch results page in your browser. Launch participant details are cached in browser local storage for 24 hours (keyed per page URL) so repeat visits avoid refetching immediately. Nothing is sent to the author or any third party.
