parkrun Annual Summary adds year-by-year participation statistics to event history pages (e.g. [Maribyrnong event history](https://www.parkrun.com.au/maribyrnong/results/eventhistory/)).

## What you see

A sortable table of calendar years with event counts, total / average / minimum / maximum finishers and volunteers, and year-on-year growth percentages, plus charts for totals and growth.

You can compare with nearby events within about 50 km (using the public parkrun events list) and **export HTML** for a self-contained snapshot you can share with event teams for analysis, or to prompt supportive conversations about handling larger fields.

## Privacy and data

Event metadata may be cached in browser local storage for about a day. Network requests stay in your browser; nothing is sent to the author or any third party beyond loading public parkrun data.
