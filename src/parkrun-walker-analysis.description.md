parkrun Walker Analysis summarises finishers at walking pace versus faster participants on dated single-event results pages (e.g. [Piggly Wiggly results](https://www.parkrun.co.za/pigglywiggly/results/2024-06-15/)).

## Where it works

It runs on dated results URLs, not on `latestresults` alone.

## Classification

Walkers are finishers at or slower than **10:00 per kilometre**: 50:00 for a 5k, or 20:00 for junior (2k) locations whose slug includes `-juniors`. Missing times may be estimated from neighbouring finishers so they can still be classified.

## What you see

Summary breakdowns (for example volunteer experience, gender, age group, and parkrun experience), a stacked finishers-per-minute chart, and a summary table. **Save as Image** downloads the visualisation so you can share it with event teams for analysis, or to prompt supportive conversations about handling larger fields.

Result rows themselves are not restyled or highlighted.
