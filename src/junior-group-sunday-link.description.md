junior parkrun Group Sunday Link adds a shortcut on junior parkrun group pages to the consolidated club report for the most recent Sunday (e.g. [Westerfolds junior parkrun group](https://www.parkrun.com.au/westerfolds-juniors/groups/24238/)).

## What it adds

A link to `parkrun.com` consolidated club results for that club on the most recent Sunday (today’s date when today is Sunday), inserted after the existing club link.
