parkrun Charts adds visualisations on parkrun results and event history pages (e.g. [Cassiobury event history](https://www.parkrun.org.uk/cassiobury/results/eventhistory/)). Saved images are useful to share with event teams for analysis, or to prompt supportive conversations about handling larger fields.

## Results pages

A finishers-per-minute bar chart from the finish times on the page. **Save as Image** downloads the chart.

## Event history pages

A chart of finishers and volunteers over time. When there are at least twelve events, twelve-event rolling averages are included, with minimum and maximum callouts. **Save as Image** downloads the chart.
